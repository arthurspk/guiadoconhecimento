## ⭐ Where to start

> First steps with AI, for anyone: official guides, free courses, and general-purpose tools.

- [Introdução à IA generativa e aos agentes (Microsoft Learn)](https://learn.microsoft.com/pt-br/training/modules/fundamentals-generative-ai/) - Free and official Portuguese module on LLMs, writing of prompts and AI agents, with exercise and evaluation. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Introdução à IA generativa (Google Skills)](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/536?locale=pt_BR) - Free Google Microcourse, in Portuguese, 45 minutes on the foundations of AI generativa, with seal at the end. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [IA Generativa para Iniciantes (Microsoft)](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners/tree/main/translations/pt-BR) - Open course of Microsoft with 21 lessons, translated into Portuguese, from fundamentals and prompts to RAG, agents and responsible use. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Visão geral da engenharia de prompts (Claude)](https://platform.claude.com/docs/pt-BR/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview) - Anthropic's official guide in Portuguese with prompt techniques for Claude: clarity, examples, roles and chaining. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Estratégias de design de comandos (Gemini API)](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/prompting-strategies?hl=pt-br) - Official Google guide in Portuguese on how to structure instructions, examples and context into prompts. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Práticas recomendadas de engenharia de prompt (OpenAI)](https://help.openai.com/pt-br/articles/6654000-best-practices-for-prompt-engineering-with-the-openai-api) - OpenAI official article, translated into Portuguese, with practical rules for writing more effective prompts. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Curso de LLMs da Hugging Face (português)](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/pt/chapter1/1) - Free course to understand how Transformer models work and the ecosystem of open models, with chapters in Portuguese. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com) - Free introductory course from the University of Helsinki on what is AI, without requiring programming; has Portuguese version. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Anthropic Academy](https://anthropic.skilljar.com) - Anthropic courses on fluency in AI, Claude, Claude Code, MCP and Agent Skills with certificate. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com) - OpenAI learning platform with lessons and events on practical use of AI at work. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Guia de Engenharia de Prompt](https://www.promptingguide.ai/pt) - Open and translated guide to Portuguese with prompt techniques, agents and risks of language models. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Tutorial interativo de prompts da Anthropic](https://github.com/anthropics/prompt-eng-interactive-tutorial) - Practical and free course on notebooks, step by step, to write better prompts.
- [DeepLearning.AI Short Courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/) - Short courses on generativa AI, agents and RAG made with companies in the sector; many free. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Hugging Face Learn](https://huggingface.co/learn) - Free courses of LLMs, agents, computer vision, diffusion and more with open source practice. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai) - Independent comparison of AI models and providers by quality, speed, cost and latency to choose which one to use.
