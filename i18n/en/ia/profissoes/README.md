## 🔎 How the list is built

> The selection is automatic and reproducible; nothing is written by hand.

- The queries for each profession live in `data/repos-ia-consultas.json`, grouped by theme.
- The `scripts/coletar_repos_ia.py` script queries GitHub's public search and sorts by stars.
- Only active repositories make the cut: those with a description, a clear AI signal in the name, description, or topics, and not on a blocklist (adult content, piracy, cheating).
- Star counts are from the day of collection. A repository can appear under more than one profession.
