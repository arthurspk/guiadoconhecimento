### 💎 AI essentials

- [Recursos de IA e seu site (Google Search Central)](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features?hl=pt-br) - Official documentation on how AI Overviews and AI Mode use and quote websites and what to do (and not do) to appear in them. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Orientações do Google sobre conteúdo gerado com IA](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content?hl=pt-br) - Google's official position: content made with AI is accepted if it is useful and original, and use to manipulate ranking violates spam policies. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
