- [AI Act da UE, Anexo III (sistemas de alto risco)](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/) - Official list commented that classifies AI for recruitment, selection, promotion and dismissal as high risk. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [NYC Local Law 144 (ferramentas automatizadas de contratação)](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/automated-employment-decision-tools.page) - New York law requiring bias audit and warning candidates in the use of AI in hiring, good practice reference. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>

- [PL 2338/2023 (Marco Legal da IA) no Senado](https://www25.senado.leg.br/web/atividade/materias/-/materia/157233) - Official document of the Brazilian bill for regulation of AI, approved by the Senate in Dec/2024 and referred to the House on Mar/2025. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
