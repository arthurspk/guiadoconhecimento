## ⭐ A taste of each area

> The top essentials from each area in this sector, so you can get a feel for what each page offers.

### 🖌️ UI/UX Design

- [Figma](https://www.figma.com/) - Market standard collaborative tool for interface design, prototyping and handoff with free plan.
- [Penpot](https://penpot.app/) - Alternative open source to Figma, runs in the browser and can be self-hosted.
- [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) - Collection of the main laws and heuristics of psychology applied to interface design.
- [Nielsen Norman Group - Artigos](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/) - Articles and videos based on usability research, UX research and interaction design.
- [Material Design 3](https://m3.material.io/) - Google design system with guidelines, components and tokens for interfaces. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>

→ [See all 193 links for UI/UX Design](./design-ui-ux.md)

### 🖍️ Graphic Design and Illustration

- [GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/) - Free and free image editor for retouching, composition and graphic creation.
- [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/) - Open source vector graphics editor, alternative to Illustrator.
- [Krita](https://krita.org/) - Open source digital painting and illustration program.
- [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com/) - Image editor in the browser compatible with PSD, AI, Figma and other formats.
- [Affinity](https://www.affinity.studio/) - Professional suite design, photo and diagraming, free for individual use.

→ [See all 213 links for Graphic Design and Illustration](./design-grafico.md)

### 🎞️ Video Editing

- [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) - Professional editor with editing, color grading, VFX and audio in one tool with free version.
- [Treinamento oficial DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/training) - PDF books and free Blackmagic video classes, with several modules in Portuguese. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/) - KDE open source video editor with multicam, color grading and automatic subtitles.
- [Shotcut](https://www.shotcut.org/) - Free and multiplatform video editor with native editing of various formats.
- [OpenShot](https://www.openshot.org/) - Simple open source video editor for beginners, with keyframes and effects.

→ [See all 209 links for Video Editing](./edicao-de-video.md)

### 🎥 Filmmaking and Cinema

- [StudioBinder - Blog](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/) - Detailed guides on script, camera language, direction and production.
- [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/) - News, tutorials and podcast about making movies independently.
- [Lessons from the Screenplay](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCErSSa3CaP_GJxmFpdjG9Jw) - Video-testing channel that analyzes screenplays of known films. <sub>📺 channel</sub>
- [American Cinematographer (ASC)](https://theasc.com/) - Magazine and website of the American Society of Cinematographers on photography direction.
- [Fountain](https://fountain.io/) - Simple text marking language for writing scripts in standard format. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>

→ [See all 149 links for Filmmaking and Cinema](./filmmaking.md)

### 🪄 Audiovisual Resources

- [Lutify.me Free LUTs](https://lutify.me/free-luts/) - Free package of Premiere, DaVinci Resolves, Final Cut and Photoshop compatible cinematographic LUTs.
- [Juan Melara - Print Film Emulation LUTs](https://juanmelara.com.au/blog/print-film-emulation-luts-for-download) - Free copy film emulation LUTs (Kodak 2383 and Fujifilm 3510), with explanation of how to use them. <sub>📄 research paper</sub>
- [Fresh LUTs](https://freshluts.com/) - Community Repository to seek, download and send LUTs created by other colorists.
- [ARRI LUT Generator](https://www.arri.com/en/learn-help/learn-help-camera-system/tools/lut-generator) - Official ARRI tool to generate conversion LUTs from LogC3 to set and post-production.
- [Panasonic LUT Library (V-Log)](https://av.jpn.support.panasonic.com/support/global/cs/dsc/download/lut/index.html) - Panasonic's official website with LUTs for V-Log recorded material on Lumix cameras.

→ [See all 306 links for Audiovisual Resources](./recursos-audiovisuais.md)

### 📷 Photography

- [darktable](https://www.darktable.org/) - RAW developer and open source photo manager, alternative to Lightroom.
- [RawTherapee](https://www.rawtherapee.com/) - Open source RAW file processor with 32-bit engine and advanced controls.
- [digiKam](https://www.digikam.org/) - Open source photo collection manager with facial recognition and advanced search.
- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) - High quality photo bank with free license.
- [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/) - Free bank of photos and videos, with interface in Portuguese.

→ [See all 187 links for Photography](./fotografia.md)

### 🧊 Motion Design, 3D, and Animation

- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) - Free 3D suite for modeling, animation, simulation, rendering, composition and editing.
- [Manual do Blender](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/) - Official Blender documentation, with translations into several languages. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Blender Studio](https://studio.blender.org/) - Courses, open films and production files from the Blender Foundation, with part per subscription. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Curso de Blender 5.1 para iniciantes (Allan Brito)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KAxWqMiKpQ) - Free course of about 4 hours in Portuguese from Brazil, released by BlenderNation. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) - Free textures, HDRIs and 3D models in the public domain.

→ [See all 212 links for Motion Design, 3D, and Animation](./motion-3d.md)

### 🎧 Audio, Music, and Podcasting

- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/) - Free audio editor for recording, cutting, cleaning noise and exporting audio.
- [REAPER](https://www.reaper.fm/) - Light and complete DAW with cheap license and extended free evaluation.
- [Ardour](https://ardour.org/) - DAW open source for multi-track recording, editing and mixing.
- [LMMS](https://lmms.io/) - Free music production station with sequencer, synthesizers and piano roll.
- [BandLab](https://www.bandlab.com/) - Free platform to produce music on the browser and mobile, owner of Cakewalk.

→ [See all 215 links for Audio, Music, and Podcasting](./audio-musica.md)

### ✍️ Writing

- [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/) - Grammar and style broker with Brazilian Portuguese support.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) - Highlights long sentences and confusing constructions to make the text clearer.
- [Google Technical Writing](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing) - Free Google courses of technical writing for developers and writers. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Google Developer Documentation Style Guide](https://developers.google.com/style) - Google style guide for clear and consistent technical documentation. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Write the Docs](https://www.writethedocs.org/) - Global documentation community with guide, conferences and Slack. <sub>👥 community</sub>

→ [See all 211 links for Writing](./escrita.md)
