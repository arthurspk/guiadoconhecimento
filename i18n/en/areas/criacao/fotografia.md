## 🤖 AI for Photography

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Photography. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [Adobe Lightroom](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html) - Photo editor with AI for assisted selection, subject masks and sky, dust removal, lens blur, adaptive presets and generative upscale; paid subscription.
- [Topaz Photo](https://www.topazlabs.com/topaz-photo) - AI application to remove noise, recover sharpness and faces and zoom images as well as Lightroom plugin and Photoshop; paid subscription.
- [Aftershoot](https://aftershoot.com/) - Automate selection (culling), editing in its style and retouching in batch, processing offline; 30-day test and plans from US$ 10/month.
- [Imagen AI](https://imagen-ai.com/) - Learn your editing style from the Lightroom catalog and apply in whole trials, as well as automatic cooking and retouching; free trial and paid plans.
- [Luminar Neo](https://skylum.com/luminar) - Photo editor with AI tools for sky, skin, object removal and enhancement as well as plugin; perpetual license paid with 7 day test.
- [Upscayl](https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl) - Desktop application open source and free to zoom photos with IA (Real-ESRGAN) locally, on Windows, macOS and Linux.
- [IOPaint](https://github.com/Sanster/IOPaint) - Open source inpainting tool for erasing objects, people, brands and photo defects using local AI models.
- [rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) - Library and CLI open source that removes the background of batch images, useful for product photos and clippings.
- [Immich](https://immich.app/) - Photo and video self-hosted manager and open source with facial recognition and intelligent AI search, without sending the collection to third parties.
- [exif-mcp](https://github.com/stass/exif-mcp) - MCP server that allows AI agents to read EXIF, XMP and GPS metadata to analyze and organize photo libraries.
- [Topaz MCP](https://github.com/TopazLabs/topaz-mcp) - Topaz Labs official MCP server to enlarge, reduce noise and increase image sharpness by the Topaz API from AI agents; paid API.
- [Content Credentials](https://contentcredentials.org/) - Open initiative based on the C2PA standard to record and verify source and photo editing, including use of AI.
- [U.S. Copyright Office: Copyright and Artificial Intelligence](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) - Central U.S. Copyright Office with reports on AI-based work registration and use of works in model training. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
