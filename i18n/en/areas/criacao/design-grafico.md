## 🤖 AI for Graphic Design and Illustration

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Graphic Design and Illustration. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [Adobe Firefly](https://firefly.adobe.com/) - Adobe AI Studio to generate and edit images, vectors, video and audio with own models and partners; limited free credits and paid plans.
- [Photoshop: Preenchimento Generativo](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop/generative-fill.html) - Photoshop AI feature page that adds, removes and expands content by prompt with partner template option; requires subscription.
- [Canva AI Connector (MCP)](https://www.canva.com/help/mcp-agent-setup/) - Official help to connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot to the Canva account and create or edit designs by conversation; free of charge on all plans, some paid features. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Midjourney (documentação)](https://docs.midjourney.com/) - Official Midjourney documentation with parameters, style and character references and good prompt practices; the service is paid for. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Ideogram](https://ideogram.ai/) - Strong image generator in readable typography inside the picture, useful for posters, logos and text parts; it has free use and paid API.
- [Recraft](https://www.recraft.ai/) - Image generator aimed at designers who create editable vectors (SVG), custom mockups and brand styles; free plan and paid plans.
- [ComfyUI](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI) - Open source interface based on nodes to run Stable Diffusion, FLUX and other image, video and audio models locally with full flow control; docs.comfy.org documentation.
- [ComfyUI MCP Server (joenorton/comfyui-mcp-server)](https://github.com/joenorton/comfyui-mcp-server) - MCP server that lets agents like Claude generate and refine images, audio and video using a local instance of ComfyUI.
- [Krita AI Diffusion](https://github.com/Acly/krita-ai-diffusion) - Open source plugin that leads generation, inpainting and sketch/pose control to Krita using local or cloud ComfyUI; good for illustrators.
- [InvokeAI](https://github.com/invoke-ai/InvokeAI) - Open source studio of image generation with unified canvas, layers and regional control, designed for professional flow (Apache 2.0 license).
- [canvas-design (skill da Anthropic)](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/canvas-design) - Claude's official Skill to create posters and static visual pieces in PNG/PDF from a design philosophy without copying styles of artists.
- [Curso de modelos de difusão (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/learn/diffusion-course) - Free and open course that explains how the diffusion models behind Stable Diffusion work, with practical notebooks in PyTorch. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Glaze (Universidade de Chicago)](https://glaze.cs.uchicago.edu/) - Free tool that applies almost invisible disturbances in digital arts to make it difficult to imitate style by AI models.
