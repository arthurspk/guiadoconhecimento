### 💎 AI essentials

- [Blender MCP](https://github.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp) - MCP open source server that connects Claude to Blender to create and modify scenes, objects and materials by natural language.
- [After Effects MCP](https://github.com/Dakkshin/after-effects-mcp) - MCP server that allows AI assistants to create compositions, layers and animations in Adobe After Effects.
- [Remotion Agent Skills](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills) - Official Removal Skills for agents to create motion graphics and programmatic videos in React, with subtitles, animations and render.
- [Cascadeur](https://cascadeur.com/) - 3D animation software of characters with AI for automatic poses, physics and intermediation between keyframes; free without commercial use, paid licenses.
- [Meshy](https://www.meshy.ai/) - Generates 3D models with PBR texture, rig and animation from text or image, with export to FBX, GLB and STL; free and paid plan.
- [Tripo AI](https://www.tripo3d.ai/) - 3D model generator from text or image, with mesh optimized for engines, texture and auto-rig; free and paid plan.
- [Spline AI 3D Generation](https://spline.design/ai) - Generates textured 3D objects from text or direct image on Spline, ready for interactive scenes on the web.
- [Rokoko Vision](https://www.rokoko.com/products/vision) - Capture of motion by AI from common video, exporting FBX or BVH to Blender, Unreal and Unity; small free quota and paid plans.
- [Move AI](https://www.move.ai/) - Motion capture without markers from common cameras, focused on VFX studios, games and advertising; paid service.
- [Hunyuan3D 2.1](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1) - Open Tencent model that generates 3D meshes with PBR textures from images; own community license with territory restrictions and use.
- [TRELLIS.2 (Microsoft)](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2) - Microsoft open source (MIT) model to generate high-quality 3D assets from images.
- [LivePortrait](https://github.com/KwaiVGI/LivePortrait) - Open source (MIT) model that animates portraits from a reference video, transferring expressions and head movements.
