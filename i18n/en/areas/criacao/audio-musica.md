### 💎 AI essentials

- [Demucs](https://github.com/adefossez/demucs) - Open source model of stem separation (voice, battery, bass and others) reference, maintained by the original author after archiving in Meta repository.
- [python-audio-separator](https://github.com/nomadkaraoke/python-audio-separator) - Open source library and CLI to separate stems with UVR models (MDX-Net, Roformer, Demucs), easy to automate.
- [Ultimate Vocal Remover (UVR)](https://github.com/Anjok07/ultimatevocalremovergui) - Free application with graphical interface that brings together various AI models to extract voice and music instruments locally.
- [Moises](https://moises.ai/) - App for musicians who separate instruments and voice, detect chords and movement and help to study and rehearse; limited free plan and signature.
- [LALAL.AI](https://www.lalal.ai/) - Voice and instrument separation service, noise and echo removal, with apps, VST plugin and API; free preview and paid plans.
- [iZotope RX](https://www.izotope.com/en/products/rx.html) - Industry standard for audio restoration with machine learning modules to isolate dialogue, remove noise and separate stems; paid.
- [Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech](https://podcast.adobe.com/enhance) - Free Adobe tool that cleans voice recordings to sound like recorded in treated studio.
- [Auphonic](https://auphonic.com/) - Automatic audio post-production with AI (leveling, loudness, noise reduction, transcription) for podcasts; 2 free hours per month.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) - Synthetic voice platform, voice dubbing and cloning in more than 70 languages; limited free plan, cloning requires consent from the owner of the voice.
- [Suno](https://suno.com/) - Generates complete songs with voice and lyrics from text; free plan without commercial rights, which require paid plan.
- [ACE-Step](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step) - Open source (Apache 2.0) model of music generation with lyrics, style and editing of snippets, runs locally.
- [Whisper (OpenAI)](https://github.com/openai/whisper) - Open source model of transcription and speech translation, basis for transcribing podcasts and interviews in Portuguese and other languages.
- [Ableton MCP](https://github.com/ahujasid/ableton-mcp) - MCP server that connects Claude to Ableton Live to create tracks, MIDI clips and manipulate the session by natural language.
- [Curso de áudio da Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/learn/audio-course) - Free course on Transformers applied to audio: speech recognition, audio rating and voice synthesis. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Spotify: proteções contra abuso de IA na música](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-09-25/spotify-strengthens-ai-protections/) - Official announcement of Spotify rules on voice imitation by AI, spam filter and credits that declare the use of AI in tracks.
