### 💎 AI essentials

- [Uma (assistente de IA da Upwork)](https://www.upwork.com/uma) - AI Assistant embedded in the Upwork that helps freelancers write proposals and clients to write vacancies; always review the text before sending.
- [DeepL Tradutor](https://www.deepl.com/pt-BR/translator) - Translator with AI widely used to serve foreign customers; free version with limits and paid Pro plan more privacy.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) - English writing assistant with AI for e-mails and proposals to international customers; free plan with basic revision and prompt quota.
- [Fathom](https://www.fathom.ai/) - Free AI meeting notifier, recording, transcribing and summarizing calls in Zoom, Google Meet and Teams.
- [tl;dv](https://tldv.io/pt-br/) - Assistant meetings with AI that records, transcribes and summarizes calls in Portuguese; permanent free plan. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai) - Transcription and automatic summary of meetings with free plan; useful to register combined with customers (watch the participants before recording).
- [Gamma](https://gamma.app) - Generate presentations, proposals and pages from text with AI; free plan with initial credits.
- [Loom AI](https://www.loom.com/ai) - Loom AI features for asynchronous videos (titles, abstracts, chapters and pause removal); available in the paid plan Business + AI.
