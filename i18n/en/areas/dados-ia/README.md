### 📊 Data Analysis and BI

- [Documentação do Power BI](https://learn.microsoft.com/pt-br/power-bi/) - Official documentation in Portuguese of Power BI, from Desktop to cloud service. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Certificação PL-300 (Power BI Data Analyst)](https://learn.microsoft.com/pt-br/credentials/certifications/data-analyst-associate/) - Official website of the Power BI data analyst certification, with free study guide and practical evaluation. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [DAX Guide](https://dax.guide/) - Full reference of DAX language functions and operators used in Power BI and Excel. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Fundação Bradesco - Escola Virtual](https://www.ev.org.br/) - Free online courses with certificate, including Excel from basics to advanced. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Hashtag Treinamentos](https://www.youtube.com/@HashtagTreinamentos) - Brazilian channel with classes of Excel, Power BI, SQL and Python for the labor market. <sub>📺 channel · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>

### 🔀 Data Engineering

- [Data Engineering Zoomcamp](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/data-engineering-zoomcamp) - Free and open course building a complete pipeline with Docker, Terraform, dbt, Spark and Kafka. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [roadmap.sh - Data Engineer](https://roadmap.sh/data-engineer) - Interactive script than studying to become a data engineer.
- [Microsoft Learn - Engenheiro de Dados](https://learn.microsoft.com/pt-br/training/career-paths/data-engineer) - Official and free Portuguese trail with data engineering modules in Azure. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net/) - Martin Kleppmann's book on the fundamentals of distributed data systems, storage and streaming. <sub>📚 book</sub>
- [Big Book of Data Engineering](https://www.databricks.com/resources/ebook/big-book-of-data-engineering) - Databricks free ebook with patterns and practical cases of pipelines and lakehouse. <sub>📚 book</sub>

→ [See all 210 links for Data Engineering](./engenharia-de-dados.md)

### 🧠 Data Science and Machine Learning

- [Machine Learning Specialization (Andrew Ng)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/machine-learning-introduction) - Classic ML introductory course from Stanford and DeepLearning.AI, with free registration option. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Machine Learning Crash Course (Google)](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course) - Google's free and practical course with videos, interactive views and exercises. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Practical Deep Learning for Coders (fast.ai)](https://course.fast.ai/) - Free Deep Learning Course applied to those who already program, with PyTorch and fastai. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [An Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/) - Reference book in statistical learning, with free PDF and editions in R and Python. <sub>📚 book</sub>
- [Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/) - Interactive and free deep learning book with executable code in PyTorch, TensorFlow and JAX. <sub>📚 book</sub>

### ✨ Generative AI and LLMs

- [Generative AI for Beginners (Microsoft)](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners) - Free course in 21 lessons on apps with generativa AI, with translation into Portuguese. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Hugging Face Learn](https://huggingface.co/learn) - Free courses of LLMs, agents, diffusion and context engineering. <sub>🎓 course</sub>

### 🗃️ Datasets and Open Data

- [Portal Brasileiro de Dados Abertos](https://dados.gov.br/) - Official open data catalogue of the Brazilian federal government. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Base dos Dados](https://basedosdados.org/) - Public datalake with hundreds of Brazilian databases treated and ready for SQL, Python and R. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Brasil.IO](https://brasil.io/) - Repository of open format Brazilian public data, such as companies, salaries and elections. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [IBGE](https://www.ibge.gov.br/) - Portal of the official institute of statistics and geography in Brazil. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [SIDRA - IBGE](https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/) - IBGE system to consult and download research tables, censuses and indexes. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
