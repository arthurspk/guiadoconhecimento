## ⭐ Start here

> The essentials for Datasets and Open Data, hand-picked by the curators: if you only have time for a few links, these are the ones.

- [Portal Brasileiro de Dados Abertos](https://dados.gov.br/) - Official open data catalogue of the Brazilian federal government. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Base dos Dados](https://basedosdados.org/) - Public datalake with hundreds of Brazilian databases treated and ready for SQL, Python and R. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Brasil.IO](https://brasil.io/) - Repository of open format Brazilian public data, such as companies, salaries and elections. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [IBGE](https://www.ibge.gov.br/) - Portal of the official institute of statistics and geography in Brazil. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [SIDRA - IBGE](https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/) - IBGE system to consult and download research tables, censuses and indexes. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Portal da Transparência](https://portaldatransparencia.gov.br/) - Data on spending, contracts, servers and benefits of the federal government, with open download. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Dados Abertos do TSE](https://dadosabertos.tse.jus.br/) - Official election data: candidates, electorate, results and accountability. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Microdados do INEP](https://www.gov.br/inep/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/dados-abertos/microdados) - Microdata from ENEM, School Census and other educational evaluations. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [DATASUS](https://datasus.saude.gov.br/) - Department of informatics of SUS, source of data and health information systems. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Dados Abertos do Banco Central](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/) - Interest series, exchange rate, credit and payments from the Central Bank with API. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Dados Abertos da Câmara dos Deputados](https://dadosabertos.camara.leg.br/) - API and archives on MPs, votes and legislative proposals. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Querido Diário](https://github.com/okfn-brasil/querido-diario) - Open Knowledge Brazil project that collects and releases official municipal diaries. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Kaggle Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets) - Hundreds of thousands of public datasets with community notebooks.
- [Hugging Face Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets) - Hub with over a million datasets for machine learning and AI.
- [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/) - Classic datasets repository for ML teaching and research.
- [Google Dataset Search](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/) - Google Searcher specializing in finding web-published datasets.
- [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/) - Open data and graphs on health, poverty, climate, education and the world economy.
- [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/) - Development indicators from all countries, with free access.
- [data.europa.eu](https://data.europa.eu/) - Portal that aggregates more than one million open datasets from European countries and institutions.

### 💎 AI essentials

- [SQL Console do Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/datasets-viewer-sql-console) - Browser Console (DuckDB) to view Hub datasets in SQL or natural language and export results. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Corpus Carolina](https://huggingface.co/datasets/carolina-c4ai/corpus-carolina) - Corpus opened from contemporary Brazilian Portuguese of USP/C4AI, with more than 2 million documents and registered provenance; CC BY 4.0. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Chatbot da Base dos Dados](https://basedosdados.org/chatbot-lp) - Chatbot with Gemini that transforms questions into queries about more than 740 Brazilian public tables; free test of 7 days, then signature. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Croissant (MLCommons)](https://mlcommons.org/working-groups/data/croissant/) - Open metadata pattern for ML datasets, used by Hugging Face, Kaggle and Google Dataset Search. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Datasheets for Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010) - Reference article that proposes documenting motivation, composition, collection and recommended uses of all ML dataset. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Data Provenance Initiative](https://www.dataprovenance.org) - Research initiative that audits origin, licenses and restrictions of the data used to train AI models.
- [Common Crawl](https://commoncrawl.org) - Open and free web tracking archive with hundreds of billion pages, the basis of many LLMs corpora.
- [SDV (Synthetic Data Vault)](https://github.com/sdv-dev/SDV) - Python library to generate synthetic tabular data from real data, useful for testing and safe sharing.
- [distilabel](https://github.com/argilla-io/distilabel) - Open source framework to generate and evaluate synthetic datasets with LLMs (instructions, preferences, evaluation).
- [Argilla](https://github.com/argilla-io/argilla) - Open source platform for curatorship and data annotation with LLM support and human feedback.
- [Label Studio](https://github.com/HumanSignal/label-studio) - Open source tool for labeling text, image, audio and video with pre-notation by ML models.
