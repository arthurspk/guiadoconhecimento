## ⭐ A taste of each area

> The top essentials from each area in this sector, so you can get a feel for what each page offers.

### 🛡️ Cybersecurity

- [Cartilha de Segurança para Internet (CERT.br)](https://cartilha.cert.br/) - Free Brazilian material with fascicles on scams, passwords, malware and data protection. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [CERT.br](https://www.cert.br/) - National response to incidents in Brazil, with statistics, good practices and materials. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [OWASP Top 10](https://top10.owasp.org/) - List of most critical security risks in web applications. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [PortSwigger Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security) - Free web security training with theory and practical laboratories. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Hacker101](https://www.hacker101.com/) - HackerOne's free web security and CTF classes, aimed at bug bounty. <sub>🎓 course</sub>

### 🔎 OSINT and Investigation

- [OSINT Framework](https://github.com/lockfale/osint-framework) - Interactive map of OSINT tools organized by type of data investigated.
- [Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/) - Organization of investigative journalism that is a world reference in investigations from open sources.
- [Bellingcat Online Investigation Toolkit](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit) - Reviewed catalog of research tools, with use guides.
- [Guias da Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/category/resources/how-tos/) - Practical geolocation tutorials, satellite images and verification.
- [Abraji](https://abraji.org.br/) - Brazilian Association of Investigative Journalism, with courses and assessment guides. <sub>👥 community · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>

→ [See all 205 links for OSINT and Investigation](./osint.md)

### 🕶️ Privacy and Data Protection

- [LGPD - Lei 13.709/2018](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm) - Official and updated text of the General Personal Data Protection Act. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [ANPD](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br) - National Data Protection Authority, with guides, regulations and reporting channel. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Curso de Introdução à LGPD (Escola Virtual.Gov)](https://www.escolavirtual.gov.br/curso/153) - Open and free course of the federal government on LGPD, with certificate. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Cartilha CERT.br - Fascículos](https://cartilha.cert.br/fasciculos/) - Free fascicles about privacy, data protection, social networks and leaks. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Surveillance Self-Defense (EFF)](https://ssd.eff.org/pt-br) - EFF Guide in Portuguese to protect yourself from digital surveillance. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
