### ➗ Mathematics and Statistics

- [Khan Academy - Matemática](https://pt.khanacademy.org/math) - Free mathematics courses in Portuguese, from elementary school to calculus, with exercises. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [OBMEP](https://www.obmep.org.br/) - The Brazilian Mathematical Olympics of Public Schools, with previous evidence, questions banks and scientific initiation programs. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Portal da OBMEP](https://portaldaobmep.impa.br/) - Video classes, exercise lists and free math materials from 6th grade to high school. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [IMPA](https://impa.br/) - Institute of Pure and Applied Mathematics, reference in research and post-graduation in mathematics in Brazil. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Matemática Multimídia (Unicamp)](https://m3.ime.unicamp.br/) - Videos, audios, software and math experiments for high school produced by Unicamp. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>

### 🔭 Physics and Astronomy

- [Khan Academy - Física](https://pt.khanacademy.org/science/physics) - Free classes and exercises of physics in Portuguese. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [OBA - Olimpíada Brasileira de Astronomia](http://www.oba.org.br/) - National astronomy and astronomical Olympics for elementary school students. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Astronomia e Astrofísica (UFRGS)](http://astro.if.ufrgs.br/) - Book and website of Kepler e Saraiva (UFRGS), reference in astronomy in Portuguese. <sub>📚 book · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Revista Brasileira de Ensino de Física](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbef/) - Open access periodical of the SBF on teaching and dissemination of physics. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>

### 🧬 Biology and Bioinformatics

- [Khan Academy - Biologia](https://pt.khanacademy.org/science/biology) - Free classes and biology exercises in Portuguese. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [SiBBr](https://www.sibbr.gov.br/) - Brazilian Biodiversity Information System, which integrates data from species and ecosystems. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Flora e Funga do Brasil](https://floradobrasil.jbrj.gov.br/) - Official catalog of plants, algae and fungi in Brazil, maintained by the Botanical Garden of Rio de Janeiro. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Instituto Butantan](https://butantan.gov.br/) - Biomedical research center and production of vaccines and serums, with scientific dissemination content. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) - Biomedical and genomic database portal (GenBank, BLAST, Gene, PubMed).
