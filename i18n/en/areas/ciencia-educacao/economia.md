## 🤖 AI for Economics

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Economics. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [Generative AI for Economic Research (Korinek, JEL)](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jel.20231736) - Journal of Economic Literature article with practical use cases of LLMs in ideation, writing, data, code and mathematical derivations.
- [Applied Causal Inference Powered by ML and AI](https://causalml-book.org/) - Free book of Chernozhukov and coauthors on causal inference with machine learning, with laboratories in Python and R. <sub>📚 book</sub>
- [EconML (PyWhy)](https://github.com/py-why/EconML) - Python library to estimate heterogeneous effects of treatment with ML (double ML, causal forests); open source.
- [DoubleML](https://github.com/DoubleML/doubleml-for-py) - Python implementation of Double/Debiased Machine Learning for causal estimation in econometry; open source.
- [EconDL - Deep Learning for Economists](https://econdl.github.io/) - Melissa Dell's classes and resources on deep learning applied to the economy and social sciences; free. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/economic-index) - Data and interactive panel on how AI is being used by occupation and region, with periodic reports.
- [FMI - Gen-AI and the Future of Work](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/Staff-Discussion-Notes/Issues/2024/01/14/Gen-AI-Artificial-Intelligence-and-the-Future-of-Work-542379) - The IMF notes that it estimates the exposure of jobs to AI in advanced and emerging economies and discusses policies. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [OECD.AI / GPAI](https://oecd.ai/) - OECD and GPAI Observatory with policies, data, indicators and monitoring of AI incidents by country.
- [Stanford AI Index](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index) - Annual report with data on investment, adoption, labor market and AI policies; free.
- [Data Commons](https://datacommons.org/) - Google base that gathers global public data and allows you to consult them with questions in natural language; free.
