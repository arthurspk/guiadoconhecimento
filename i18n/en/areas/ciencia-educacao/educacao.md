## 🤖 AI for Education and Teaching

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Education and Teaching. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [UNESCO - Guia para IA generativa na educação e na pesquisa](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research) - UNESCO's first global orientation on generativa AI in schools and universities; it has a Portuguese version. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [UNESCO - Marco de competências em IA para professores](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-teachers) - It defines 15 teaching skills in AI in five dimensions and three progression levels; available Portuguese version. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [UNESCO - Marco de competências em IA para estudantes](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students) - Twelve skills to integrate AI teaching into the school curriculum; available in Portuguese. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Khanmigo](https://www.khanmigo.ai/) - Tutor and teaching assistant with Khan Academy AI who guides the student without delivering answers; free for teachers.
- [MagicSchool](https://www.magicschool.ai/) - Platform with dozens of AI tools to plan classes, create activities and evaluations; free for teachers.
- [Brisk Teaching](https://www.briskteaching.com/) - AI extension and platform to create classes, give feedback and differentiate activities within Google Docs; free plan.
- [Letrus](https://www.letrus.com/) - Brazilian program that combines AI and pedagogy to develop reading and writing, with correction of essays; for teaching networks. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Gemini for Education](https://edu.google.com/intl/ALL_br/ai/gemini-for-education/) - Google AI for schools with data protection, no cost to Workspace for Education institutions. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [IA para Educadores (Microsoft Learn)](https://learn.microsoft.com/pt-br/training/paths/ai-education/) - Free Portuguese trail with four modules on AI foundations, prompts and use in the classroom. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [TeachAI - AI Guidance for Schools Toolkit](https://www.teachai.org/toolkit) - Free kit with principles and policy models for schools adopting AI responsibly; CC BY-NC-SA license. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Code.org - Currículo de IA](https://code.org/ai) - Free AI curricula for primary and secondary education and the course AI 101 for teachers. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Claude for Education](https://claude.com/solutions/education) - Claude's resources for higher education, with learning mode that asks questions instead of giving answers and fluency courses in AI.
