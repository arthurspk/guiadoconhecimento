## 🤖 AI for Academic Research

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Academic Research. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [Elicit](https://elicit.com/) - Research assistant with AI that searches articles, extracts data in tables and generates reports with citations; free plan.
- [Consensus](https://consensus.app/) - AI search mechanism on peer-reviewed literature that summarizes what the studies indicate; freemium.
- [Ai2 Asta](https://asta.allen.ai/) - Research assistant at the Allen Institute for IA that finds, summarizes and analyzes scientific evidence in millions of articles.
- [SciSpace](https://scispace.com/) - AI assistant for literature review, PDF reading and writing with citations; free and paid plan.
- [scite](https://scite.ai/) - It shows whether each quote supports or contests a finding (Smart Citations) and answers questions based on the literature; paid with test.
- [ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai/) - Discover related articles and view citation networks and coauthorship for literature reviews; free with paid plan.
- [Gemini Notebook (antigo NotebookLM)](https://notebook.google/) - Google Tool that analyzes your own sources (PDFs, articles) and generates abstracts, questions and audios; free.
- [PaperQA](https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa) - Open source agent who answers questions with citations from scientific PDFs, with high precision RAG.
- [arXiv MCP Server](https://github.com/blazickjp/arxiv-mcp-server) - MCP server for Claude and other agents to search, read sections and generate BibTeX articles from the arXiv; open source.
- [Zotero MCP](https://github.com/54yyyu/zotero-mcp) - MCP server that connects your Zotero library to Claude and ChatGPT to search, summarize and write down references.
- [Scientific Agent Skills (K-Dense)](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-skills) - Skills for agents with literature review, scientific writing, citation management, statistics and posters; MIT.
- [SciELO - Guia de uso de ferramentas de IA](https://wp.scielo.org/wp-content/uploads/Guia-de-uso-de-ferramentas-e-recursos-de-IA-20230914.pdf) - Guide of the SciELO Network, in Portuguese, on transparency and authorship in the use of AI in scientific communication. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [COPE - Autoria e ferramentas de IA](https://publicationethics.org/cope-position-statements/ai-author) - Committee on Publication Ethics: AI cannot be an author and its use must be stated in the methods. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
