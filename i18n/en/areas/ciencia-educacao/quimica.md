## 🤖 AI for Chemistry

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Chemistry. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [IBM RXN for Chemistry](https://rxn.app.accelerate.science/rxn/) - IBM platform that uses AI to predict reaction products, plan backsynthesis and generate laboratory procedures; requires registration.
- [Rowan](https://rowansci.com/) - Cloud platform of computational chemistry with ML and physics to predict properties, conformations and protein-ligand interaction; free plan.
- [DeepChem](https://deepchem.io/) - Open deep learning library for chemistry, materials and biology, with free tutorials and book. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Chemprop](https://github.com/chemprop/chemprop) - Message-passing neural networks to predict molecular properties from SMILES; open source and widely used.
- [AiZynthFinder](https://github.com/MolecularAI/aizynthfinder) - Backsynthesis tool with neural networks and tree search to plan synthetic routes; open source (AstraZeneca).
- [REINVENT 4](https://github.com/MolecularAI/REINVENT4) - Generating molecular design framework with reinforcement learning to optimize drug candidates; open source.
- [MatterGen (Microsoft)](https://github.com/microsoft/mattergen) - Generating model to propose new inorganic materials with target properties; open source.
- [ChemCrow](https://github.com/ur-whitelab/chemcrow-public) - Agent with LLM that uses chemistry tools (RDKit, literature search, security) to solve synthesis and analysis tasks.
- [Scientific Agent Skills (K-Dense)](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-skills) - More than 170 skills for Claude and other agents covering RDKit, DeepChem, pymatgen, medicinal chemistry and molecular dynamics; MIT.
- [Política de IA da ACS Publications](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/aipolicy) - American Chemical Society rules to declare use of AI in texts, figures and peer review in chemistry journals. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
