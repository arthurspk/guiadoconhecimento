## 🤖 AI for Medicine and Health

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Medicine and Health. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [OMS - Ética e governança da IA para a saúde](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240029200) - Guide of the World Health Organization with six principles for ethical use of AI in health; free download. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [OMS - Orientações sobre grandes modelos multimodais (LMMs)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240084759) - WHO Guidelines on Generating AI in health, with recommendations for governments, developers and services; available also in Portuguese. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [Resolução CFM nº 2.454/2026](https://sistemas.cfm.org.br/normas/visualizar/resolucoes/br/2026/2454) - Norm of the Federal Council of Medicine that regulates the use of artificial intelligence in medical practice in Brazil. <sub>📖 documentation · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [OpenEvidence](https://www.openevidence.com/) - AI search mechanism that synthesizes biomedical literature to answer clinical questions with references.
- [Voa Health](https://voa.health/) - Brazilian platform that transcribes consultations, generates clinical documentation and offers evidence-based support; freemium. <sub>🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Abridge](https://www.abridge.com/) - AI environment that transforms the conversation of consultation into structured clinical note; solution paid for institutions.
- [Microsoft Dragon Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/health-solutions/clinical-workflow/dragon-copilot) - Microsoft clinical assistant for voice documentation and automation of medical, nursing and radiology tasks; paid.
- [Glass Health](https://glass.health/) - Clinical AI platform to support differential diagnosis and care plans.
- [AI for Medicine (DeepLearning.AI)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/ai-for-medicine) - Specialization in three courses on diagnostic imaging, prognosis and clinical NLP with machine learning; paid, with financial assistance. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [CONSORT-AI (EQUATOR Network)](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/consort-artificial-intelligence/) - Guidelines to report clinical trials with AI interventions; they have translation into Portuguese. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
- [FDA - Dispositivos médicos com IA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-devices) - Official and up-to-date list of FDA authorised AI medical devices, useful for checking product regulation. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
