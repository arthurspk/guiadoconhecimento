## 🤖 AI for Humanities

> AI tools, skills, MCPs, courses, prompt guides, and responsible-use resources for people working in Humanities. See also [🤖 AI for every field](../../ia/README.md).

### 💎 AI essentials

- [Transkribus](https://www.transkribus.org/) - AI platform to transcribe historical manuscripts and printed in more than 100 languages; 50 free credits per month.
- [eScriptorium](https://gitlab.com/scripta/escriptorium) - Open platform for manuscript recognition (HTR) with machine learning, used in academic projects; MIT license.
- [Kraken](https://github.com/mittagessen/kraken) - OCR/HTR motor with neural networks optimized for historical and non-Latin written documents; open source.
- [OCR4all](https://github.com/OCR4all/OCR4all) - Complete and free flow of OCR for old printed, designed for researchers without technical experience.
- [Programming Historian - Transcrição automática de grafias não latinas](https://programminghistorian.org/pt/licoes/transcricao-automatica-grafias-nao-latinas) - A peer-reviewed lesson in Portuguese about training OCR/HTR models for historical documents. <sub>🎓 course · 🇧🇷 pt-BR</sub>
- [Programming Historian - Visão computacional para as humanidades](https://programminghistorian.org/en/lessons/computer-vision-deep-learning-pt1) - Colab notebook lesson about classifying images from historical collections with deep learning. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [AI for Humanists](https://aiforhumanists.com/) - Project funded by the NH with bibliography, glossary, code tutorials and workshops on LLMs for research in humanities.
- [Introduction to Cultural Analytics & Python](https://github.com/melaniewalsh/Intro-Cultural-Analytics) - Melanie Walsh's open-course book on computational analysis of Python texts and culture, including PLN. <sub>🎓 course</sub>
- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy - Ética da IA e da Robótica](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-ai/) - Reference entry on privacy, bias, autonomy, opacity and risks of AI, revised in 2026. <sub>📖 documentation</sub>
